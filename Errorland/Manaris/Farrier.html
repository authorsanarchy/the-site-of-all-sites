The Collected Works of Farrier the Balladeer


A collection of short stories written as submissions to my schools literary magazine
or as scrpts to be performed at coffeehouse events, I loosely canonize these assorted
works as having been penned by an individual by the name of Farrier, a rogue bard who
wandered the realms of Manaris in the years after the War of the Alliance telling tall
tales in taverns and royal halls alike.
Updates to this gallery will be fairly irregular. I write these pieces as inspiration or
nessecity wills. Given how some of these pieces were written with the intent to be
performed live, I've considered adding recordings of some of the pieces, however given how I
have niether a quality microphone anot a great deal of confidence in my own voice acting talents
(as well as generla insecurties about my voice and sharing it online) these remain frimly on
my list of "projects to considered doing at an unspecified time in the futre"
