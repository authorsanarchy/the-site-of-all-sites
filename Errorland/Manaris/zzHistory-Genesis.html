The Birth of the Realms



15,000,000,000 before union

Through stories or scholarly pursuit, nearly every culture through the long history of Manaris has
tried to answer the question of how the realms came to be. Unfortunately for those dedicated to
the pursuit of truth, few consistent consensus have ever been reached by scholars or sages
concerning the genesis of all that is.


One of the few things that is agreed upon regarding the birth of the realms is the approximate
timeline of creation. The four core elemental realms of Water, Earth, Fire and Air are generally
agreed upon as being significantly older than the rest of the cosmos. As has been proven
numerous times by explorers seeking the furthest reaches of existence, the core elemental realms
are ever expanding at a fairly consistent rate. Mathematicians have used this speed of growth to
calculate an approximate center for each of the realms, as well as calculate a timeline at which
point this expansion must have begun—some fifteen billion years—for the core elemental realms
to have reached their current size.


Based on the distance between these respective centers and the borders where the elemental
realms collide, it is believed that prior to around five billion years ago, the four realms were
entirely separate entities impossible to traverse between in opposition to the current “ring model”
generally accepted by cosmologists. This line of thinking, leads to the belief that the Mortal
Realm, and the Realms of Light and Shadow—all located at the center of the ring model—came
into being as a result of the first collisions between the core elemental realms, the force of the
impact enough to create the two new elements of light and shadow as well as the Mortal Realm
forged from all six elements. The Beyond is also believed to have begun its existence around
time, with the birth and death of the first souls in the Mortal Realm.


As many inquisitive minds are quick to realize, the proposed timeline of existence brings about
just as many questions as it answers. While few disagree with the notion that the Mortal Realm
was born from the Elemental Realms, none have yet been able to explain where the elemental
realms came from to begin with. Many faiths across the realms posit that they came about by
means of an omnipotent creator; beings such as Vordani in the Prime Faith or the Elder Titan
from the Children of the Mountains. These faiths vary in their approach to the collision of the
elements, some seeing it as an act of curiosity, otters as an act of intentional creation.


Those who remain sceptical about the existence of a benevolent creator have put forward a
number of theories as to the origin of the elements. Some of the most prominent include the
elements as having arrived as an incursion from an outside cosmos, remnants of a long lost
cosmos that came before or simply as fundamental features of existence, that have been and will
continue to be for as long as time continues. These theories remain entirely within the realm of
speculation however, most being difficult, if not downright impossible to prove. Unfortunately
for those committed to the pursuit of knowledge, there are some mysteries to existence that may
never be solved for certain, and the origins of the world as we know it remains stubbornly chief
of the unknowable things.
