The Vigilante of Vordanya

A dark fantasy story set in the world of Manaris, if all goes to plan, The Vigilante of Vordanya
will one day be published as a proper novel. If and when the book comes out, I will be sure to add
links here for where it can be found. Until then, I'll infreqeuntly be updating this page with
character art and other previews.


<

The city is but a graveyard now. Its proud walls and prouder people are nothing but
stones and bones. But how does a city fall? How does a bastion of life and
civilization become naught but a hollow memory?

The story of the fall of Vordanya is one of vanity. Of the vapid yet venerated,
blinded by their own righteousness and pride, doomed to fall under the weight of
their arrogance.

The story of the fall of Vordanya is one of violence. Of the bloody crusades of
vengeance against the vain, a vengeance titled ‘justice’ which consumed the city in
its cycle of rage.

The story of the fall of Vordanya is one of villainy. One of cold, cruel, calculating
bastards who sought to win from the violence and the vanity, leaving a trail of
victims behind them to feed their own selfish gains.

The story of the fall of Vordanya is the story of a vigilante and others of his ilk.
Fools, the lot of them, vain, violent and vile in their own ways all believing they
could fix a broken city without fixing their own broken souls.


A horse-drawn carriage clatters down a winding cobblestone street and
the story unravels from there.
