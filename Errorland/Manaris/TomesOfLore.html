Tomes of Lore


The realms of Manaris are a vast and storied place. From the raging inferno of the Realm of Fire
to the vibrant overgrowth of the Realm of Fey to the unknowable heavens of the Beyond, for
most it would take a lifetime to even scratch the surface of the mysteries of the realms.
Fortunately, as an immortal, I happen to have lifetimes to spare. As the primary keeper of one of
the best preserved libraries of records, tales and legends collected from friends, enemies and long
dead strangers of the past, I have found myself in a unique position to collect and disseminate the
vast knowledge within my collection. Knowing everything there is to know about the world is
quite simply an impossible task, though that’s never stopped anyone from trying, least of all me,
which is where these tomes come from. Call it the boredom of immortality or the altruistic hope
to aid the future scholars to come or simply a madwoman’s pursuit of an impossible goal, within
these pages I one day hope to put to paper everything I know of this world, in as comprehensible
a manner as can be managed.

- Ervalin Shellwynd, Last Muse of the Autumn Court
