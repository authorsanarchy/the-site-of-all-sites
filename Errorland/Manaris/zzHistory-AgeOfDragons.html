The Age of Dragons



50,000-15,000 before union

The tales of the great empires of the dragons are still told throughout the realms today.
Depending on the biases of teller, legends speak of a million year long reign which uplifted the
other species it touched or else a violent and tyrannical society of rising and falling dynasties,
each dedicated to domination through strength. The truth of the matter, as is often the case, likely
lies somewhere in between.


Civilization as we know it is thought to have arisen some 50,000 years before union with
the discovery of agriculture. While nearly every species claims to be responsible for this
advancement, dragons certainly became the primary beneficiary. The first draconic communes
are thought to have consisted of singular family units, serviced by slaves from a wide variety of
species including humans, dwarves, gnomes and in rare cases giants. As technology advanced
and farming became more efficient with the help of simple tools, communes grew into small
cities home to upwards of 100 dragons and several thousand slaves.


As of 40,000 years before union, these draconic city-states had begun both warring and
trading with one another. Metals such as iron and bronze are thought to have initially been
harnessed to create weapons and armour for slave soldiers while mining operations began
amassing quantities of gold and silver to be used as currency for trade. This ever expanding
network saw dragons and their slave species settle nearly every corner of Manaris.


While their slaves worked their fields and fought their wars, dragons are thought to have
lived in reasonable comfort, with a number devoting their lives to unlocking the secrets of
arcanum. As of 30,000 years before union, magic had become ubiquitous among both dragons
and their slave species, as constructions began on the great sky cities—bastions of draconic
might that stood strong for many millennia.
