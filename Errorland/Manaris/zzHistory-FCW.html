The Fey Civil War



Around 20,000 before union

Prior to first contact with the Mortal Realm, the conflict between the Vey and the Dai in
the Realm of Light was a bitter, if bloodless schism. Fueled by differing views on the truest form
of beauty, the two sides of the conflict would take turns fashioning great works of art only for the
other side to tear them down in an endless cycle of creation and destruction. It was only after the
first contact with the Mortal Realm that the fey even became aware of the concept of death and
finality.


Following the birth of the twins Viala and Diala to Vey and Dai parents, the conflict
between the two cultures of the Realm of Light was thought to be resolved, following the
appointment of the twins as co-rulers of the realm, bringing about an era of tentative cooperation
between the two peoples. This peace was not without its tensions however, with both sides ever
seeking power over the other culminating in the assasiantion of Diala by rogue Dai elf. The exact
motivations of the assassin differ across tellings, some believing he opposed the perceived
matriarchal system of power while others claim he believed the Vey remained ever the favorite
faction between the two queens. What isn’t debated about the assasination is the result; the
creation of rifts between the Realm of Light and the Realm of Shadow, and the beginning of the
Fey Civil War.


As the conflict exists only in legend now, an exact timeline of the war is difficult to
capture. The earliest battles are said to have taken place in the city of Veydairana, the capital city
built by the diarchs, which was destroyed and forever hallowed by the death wrought within its
streets. The first hags are said to have equally been created during the conflict, when a group of
Dai experimenting with shadow magic forever cursed a Vey clan into hideousness. In retaliation,
the Dai responsible were transformed into the first goblins and banished to the Mortal Realm as
slaves to the dragons, stripped of the long lifespans enjoyed by the elves who had chosen a
mortal life.


After an uncounted number of years of fighting, and numerous attempts on her own life,
Viala supposedly brought the conflict to an end through her ascension to divinity, said to have
been aided by the souls of both her sister Diala and ‘grandparent’ Baramin. Unable to overcome
the binary thinking of the Vey and the Dai, the three new gods established the Four Perennial
Courts.
