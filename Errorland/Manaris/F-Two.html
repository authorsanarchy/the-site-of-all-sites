The Flyting of the Balladeers Two



Balladeer 1: To all great people gathered here, I bid you each lend me an ear, come near to hear the words of a balladeer. I’ll regale you with a tale of dragon red of scale who drove all who saw him pale.

Balladeer 2: A dragon? Such an old trope. I’d expect nothing less from a sorry dope. You want to entertain us? You haven't a hope, spitting your old stories I can barely cope.

Balladeer 1: My good sir, you’re causing quite the stir. You spout these words meant to hurt and yet I’m sure I can't concur. For such a bold claim I’d expect some more grandeur.

Balladeer 2: You want to talk to me of grandeur wearing that gaudy little hat? You simply can't wear such a thing and tell us you're all that. Are you a real bard? Prove it! Or else you’d better move it!

Balladeer 1: A contest? Well I won’t protest, I’m sure I’ll put this quickly to rest, after all I know that I’m the best. You won’t call me pretender when I render you like beef.

Balladeer 2: Oh we’ll see. It’ll fill me with so much glee to see you fall in infamy! You come up here smelling like pee. You want our praise, you'll have to plead.

Balladeer 1: Like pee? Is that the best you’ve got? Come on, give it some thought. If you want us all to think you're hot you’ll need to reassess what you’ve brought.

Balladeer 2: You’ll find that I’ve brought quite a lot. It seems a lesson must be taught. You're no big shot. Your face is covered with ugly spots, you’re choking like you're filled with snot, I think your tenures rather fraught now get off stage, before you make us all distraught.

Balladeer 1: Well that was rather rude.
Balladeer 2: I’m sorry isn’t this a feud? I thought that you knew that you looked like a newt.

Balladeer 1: Oh come on we know that's not true, besides your one to talk with that updo. By my view it's all askew.

Balladeer 2: You insult my hair? That takes some dare, we all know your balding under there.

Balladeer 1: At least I don’t give us all a scare, when you first walked in I thought you were a bear, crawling out of its lair. Get back in your chair, this duel isn’t fair.

Balladeer 2: Oh you think you’ve won? I’m far from done. When this is over I’ll be the only one having fun. Come on, make a pun. Let me guess, you’ve got none?

Balladeer 1: A pun? I’ve got some, but to use them would be such a waste, on your ears they’d be disgraced. You're wasting these fine peoples' time. I bet you're already running out of rhyme.

Balladeer 2: Oh I’m not done till I say so, so just give up now then we can both go, I’ve gotten rather tired of this show.

Balladeer 1: Clearly you won't concede defeat, even though to beat me you’d have to cheat. But I suppose if it must be proved, we’ll turn to them. Dear people, choose! Cheer for the one to win and boo for the one to lose!
