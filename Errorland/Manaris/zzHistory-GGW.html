The Great Green War



Around 20,000 before union

The first known contact between two distinct realms of Manaris—an event which would
come to spark a wave of cross-realm exploration and migration to come—is told in the myths
surrounding the gods Baramin, Viala and Diala. According to the tale—one which was never
disputed by the God of Magic during their reign—the individual who would come to be known
as Baramin accidentally created a rift between the Mortal Realm and the Realm of Light while
attempting to study the origins of magic at the base of World Tree. After deeming the rift
reasonably safe, a pair of human slaves were sent in to investigate, followed eventually by
Baramin themself, to discover the previously formless inhabitants of the realm had taken the
form of the first elves, mirroring and perfecting the shape of the humans they’d first encountered.


Shortly after Baramin’s first voyage, a pair of fey lovers, whose names have been lost to
time, came to live in the Mortal Realm seeking to escape the squabbling of their opposing Vey
and Dai cultures. The two would go on to birth the twins Viala and Diala, who would go on to
unite the opposing fey factions, ruling as diarchs before ascending to divinity as the gods of life
and death respectively. The fate of the original lovers is lost to time, though most stories claim
they died at peace in the mortal realm shortly before the ascension of their daughters, however
some tellings of the myth offer a much grimer fate, dying in childbirth, succumbing to disease or
to one the many other dooms the Mortal Realm has to offer.


It was shortly after this that the first beastfolk began to appear in the Mortal Realm. The
exact cause of their emergence remains debated to this day, however the timing of it has
suggested Fey interference.
