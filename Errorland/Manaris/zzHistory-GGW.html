The Great Green War



Around 20,000 before union

Following the Fey Civil War, a large number of elves chose to settle in the Mortal Realm,
forgoing their immortal existence under the belief that true purpose cannot be found in life
without the finality of death. This mass migration led to tensions with the existing draconic
civilization who had previously dominated the Mortal Realm uncontested and were far from
pleased with the sudden arrival of a rival species.


Tales of who struck first vary depending on which side tells the story. Elves claim their
settlement in the Mortal Realm was peaceful, and they only turned to war when the dragons
sought to enslave them. Dragons claim that the elves knowingly intruded on lands the dragons
needed for hunting, refusing all attempts to persuade them to leave peacefully, leaving the
dragons no choice but to attack.


The conflict that followed was by all accounts brief and bloody. Comfortable in their
previously uncontested rule, the dragons were unprepared for the swiftness or ferocity of the
elvish counterattack, which saw the extermination of all dragons on the continent of Singal.
Following several failed attempts by dragons to reclaim the continent, as well as several failed
naval invasions by the elves into Merinban, both sides begrudgingly signed the Green Treaty,
ceding Singal to the elves, who agreed to cease all attempts at territorial expansion provided their
existing borders were respected.
