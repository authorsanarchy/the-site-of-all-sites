The Continent of Rinhar

The fourth largest of the world’s continents, like its southerly neighbour Kafal Bir, Rinhar
is famously hot, dry and not particularly hospitable. The continent is typically divided into three
climatic regions: the Lonely Plains to the Northeast composed of temperate to arid grasslands,
the Taroma Desert to the Southwest composed of arid desert dotted with occasional shrubland
and the Red Woods to the Southeast composed of chaparral scrubland.


The continent earned its name during the Age of Dragons, derived from the draconic
word for “hunting ground.” While lacking in any permanent draconic settlements, the area is
thought to have been used largely for sport hunting by dragons from Saurin and Kafal Bir,
feasting on the various megafauna that dwelled within the Lonely Plains.


During the Age of Frost, the continent was thought to have been all but abandoned by the
surviving dragons, as well as largely ignored by the ever expanding Empire of Runes, with much
of the region’s flora and fauna succumbing to the ice age. Small clans of humans—thought to
have been first brought to the island during the Age of Dragons in a swiftly abandoned attempt at
settlement—were able to carve out a living off the scant surviving wildlife.

During the Age of Ruin, the continent was largely colonized by Merin settlers, founding
the Prime Cities which would go on to control the continent for the remainder of the age. As with
all the great empires of the Age of Fortune however, by the Age of Ruin, the Prime Cities had
been reduced to ruin, as the continent itself was torn apart during the Sundering.
